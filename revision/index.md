# Revision Guide

Coming back after a break? Choose the day you want to revisit. Each recap explains the main concepts, brings them together in a familiar example, and gives you a few questions to check your understanding.

Try explaining the example in your own words, then answer the questions. Open “Compare your reasoning” to check your answers on the same page. If a step still feels unclear, follow the link to the full lesson. You do not need to start the whole course again to work through one gap.

- **[1: People, identities, and access](day-01.md)**: Accounts, authentication, authorization, SSO, and provisioning.

- **[2: Requests and evidence](day-02.md)**: Requests, redirects, responses, and the limits of a log entry.

- **[3: Profiles and mappings](day-03.md)**: Attributes, schemas, transformations, and checking delivery.

- **[4: Sources and ownership](day-04.md)**: Profile sources, priority, attribute ownership, and contractors.

- **[5: Groups and assignments](day-05.md)**: Rules, membership, assignment paths, and Group Push.

- **[6: Active Directory](day-06.md)**: Imports, the agent, and delegated authentication.

- **[7: Authenticators and MFA](day-07.md)**: Enrollment, factors, methods, and FastPass.

- **[8: SAML](day-08.md)**: Trust, assertions, application matching, and validation.

- **[9: OIDC](day-09.md)**: Authorization Code, PKCE, tokens, and application sessions.

- **[10: SCIM](day-10.md)**: Account operations, responses, conflicts, and deactivation.

- **[11: Imports and matching](day-11.md)**: Discovery, association, reconciliation, and pagination.

- **[12: Joiners, movers, and leavers](day-12.md)**: Effective changes, lifecycle states, and checking removal.

- **[13: Policies and sessions](day-13.md)**: Rule selection, authentication requirements, and separate sessions.

- **[14: Requirements and responsibilities](day-14.md)**: Approval, ownership, least privilege, and recovery.

- **[15: Bringing it together](day-15.md)**: Investigating several problems without mixing their evidence.
