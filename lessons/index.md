# Lessons

The 15-day core course. Each one teaches its concepts through a realistic situation, then shows how to read the evidence and reason about what happened. Work them in order.


- [Day 1: People, identities, and access](day-01-people-identities-access.md)
- [Day 2: Following a request and reading evidence](day-02-requests-and-evidence.md)
- [Day 3: Profiles, attributes, and mappings](day-03-profiles-and-mappings.md)
- [Day 4: Sources, priority, and ownership](day-04-sources-and-ownership.md)
- [Day 5: Groups, rules, and application assignments](day-05-groups-and-assignments.md)
- [Day 6: AD imports and password validation](day-06-active-directory.md)
- [Day 7: Authenticators, enrollment, and MFA](day-07-authenticators-enrollment-mfa.md)
- [Day 8: SAML and application sign-in](day-08-saml.md)
- [Day 9: OIDC and the authorization code](day-09-oidc.md)
- [Day 10: SCIM and application accounts](day-10-scim.md)
- [Day 11: Imports, matching, and reconciliation](day-11-imports-and-matching.md)
- [Day 12: Joining, moving, and leaving](day-12-joiners-movers-leavers.md)
- [Day 13: Policies, sessions, and another authentication check](day-13-policies-and-sessions.md)
- [Day 14: Requirements, responsibility, and recovery](day-14-requirements-and-responsibilities.md)
- [Day 15: Investigate Northbridge as a connected system](day-15-integrated-case.md)

After the core course, [Day 16: Turn understanding into a safe practice plan](day-16-safe-practice-plan.md) is an optional bridge. Its predictions and practice record are included in the lesson; no tenant is needed to write the plan.
