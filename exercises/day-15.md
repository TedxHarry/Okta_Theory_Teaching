# Day 15: Final investigation tasks

Read the [case evidence](../assessments/final-case.md) before answering. Refer to evidence labels and separate facts, hypotheses, proposals, and completed outcomes.

Record your first attempt before opening the model answers or recap. For each ticket, write the approved outcome, the observation that supports your conclusion, the remaining unknown, and the next action with its verification. Use the facts supplied for that ticket; an earlier lesson's successful example is not a missing result in this case.

## 1. Explain the environment and priorities

Explain Northbridge's employee and contractor sourcing, source priority, the AD email exception, and delegated authentication. Distinguish assignment, SAML/OIDC sign-in, SCIM provisioning, and application sessions. Rank the tickets by the supplied impact, explaining which owners could act in parallel. Give a short overall handoff without claiming unsupplied resolutions.

## 2. Investigate Maya's move

For A, locate the first demonstrated discrepancy, state the supported cause, and propose correction and verification. Explain why successful Projects entry does not resolve the report issue. Describe the affected-population and recovery checks appropriate to the proposed mapping change.

## 3. Investigate Priya's Salesforce access

For B, explain the rule result and surviving assignment. Distinguish actual access from approved access. Identify the missing business decisions in the request and explain how different approval findings would change the action. What does the successful Expense OIDC evidence establish, and what can it not establish for Salesforce or Projects?

## 4. Investigate Daniel's SAML failure

For C, trace the successful boundaries followed by failure. Propose a supported correction and verification. Explain why neither a password reset nor creating another Salesforce account is justified by this packet.

## 5. Investigate Jordan's departure

For D, tell the complete leaver story, separating confirmed outcomes from outstanding access. Name next actions, responsible owners, and closure evidence for Projects and Expense. Explain why deleting the Okta identity or reactivating it for a retry would not establish the required correction.

## 6. Investigate the Projects conflict

For E, state what the 409 response proves and what remains unknown. Request evidence that distinguishes at least two explanations for the lookup/create discrepancy. Evaluate the proposed username change and explain the ownership checks required before linking, changing data, or creating a new record.

## 7. Interpret the historical AD incident

For F, distinguish the import, agent connectivity, credential result, and application outcome. Name the next evidence needed for the historical cause. Contrast this with a packet where the agent cannot reach a domain controller and receives no credential result. Explain why neither historical packet authorizes restoring Jordan's current access.

After your attempt, use the [answers](../self-checks/day-15.md) and [debrief and skills checklist](../assessments/self-checks/final-case.md).
