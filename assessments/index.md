# Assessments

Three checkpoints that test whether the connections between topics hold together: a foundation checkpoint, an integration checkpoint, and the final integrated case. Attempt each one before reading its debrief.

| When | Attempt | What it connects | Compare afterward |
|---|---|---|---|
| After Day 5 | [Foundation checkpoint](foundation.md) | Source ownership, mappings, groups, assignments, and evidence boundaries from Days 1–5. | [Foundation debrief](self-checks/foundation.md) |
| After Day 10 | [Integration checkpoint](integration.md) | AD password validation, MFA evidence, SAML, OIDC, and SCIM from Days 1–10. | [Integration debrief](self-checks/integration.md) |
| During Day 15, after Days 1–14 | [Final evidence packet](final-case.md) and [seven investigation tasks](../exercises/day-15.md) | Matching, lifecycle, policy and session evidence, requirements, and responsible next actions. | [Model investigation](../self-checks/day-15.md), then [skills checklist](self-checks/final-case.md) |

Use the packet's stated facts and keep independent variations separate. References and notes are allowed. Mark where they helped, compare the reasoning rather than exact wording, and revisit the linked lesson when a distinction remains unclear. No tenant access is needed.

Keep your first attempt so you can see which conclusions changed during review. After the debrief, try a changed-condition question before opening its explanation. Mark a point as independent only if you explained it without help for that point; using a reference is useful practice and belongs under with guidance.
