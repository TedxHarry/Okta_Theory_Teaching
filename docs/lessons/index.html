Lessons

The 15-day core course. Each one teaches its concepts through a realistic situation, then shows how to read the evidence and reason about what happened. Work them in order.

  • Day 1: People, identities, and access
  • Day 2: Following a request and reading evidence
  • Day 3: Profiles, attributes, and mappings
  • Day 4: Sources, priority, and ownership
  • Day 5: Groups, rules, and application assignments
  • Day 6: AD imports and password validation
  • Day 7: Authenticators, enrollment, and MFA
  • Day 8: SAML and application sign-in
  • Day 9: OIDC and the authorization code
  • Day 10: SCIM and application accounts
  • Day 11: Imports, matching, and reconciliation
  • Day 12: Joining, moving, and leaving
  • Day 13: Policies, sessions, and another authentication check
  • Day 14: Requirements, responsibility, and recovery
  • Day 15: Investigate Northbridge as a connected system

After the core course, Day 16: Turn understanding into a safe practice plan is an optional bridge. Its predictions and practice record are included in the lesson; no tenant is needed to write the plan.
