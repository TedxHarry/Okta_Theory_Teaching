Day 12: Reasoning exercises

Use the lesson's approved requirements and synthetic timelines. Devi's move and Jordan's departure have now taken effect.

For each affected system, compare the approved outcome with the observed result. Name what is complete, what remains unresolved, and the evidence needed to close that part of the case.

1. Interpret the state

A user has API status PROVISIONED. A colleague concludes that all application accounts exist. Explain the mistake by naming the system and account the status describes. Then distinguish an HR departure value, Okta DEPROVISIONED, and Projects active false.

2. Compare lifecycle actions

Explain suspension, deactivation, and deletion. Which retains Okta application assignments? Does Okta deactivation remove every group membership? Does suspension alone establish a Projects SCIM deactivation? Why is deletion not a substitute for verifying target access removal?

3. Reason about a joiner

An approved future employee record exists in HR. An existing application account is also found with a similar name. No match confirmation, effective access approval, activation result, or application-entry evidence is supplied. What decisions and evidence are needed before claiming the person is ready to work?

4. Investigate Devi's old access

Use M1 to M6. Find the first demonstrated access discrepancy against the approved move requirement. Explain why changing the Sales rule or reverting Finance would be the wrong correction. What should happen next, and what must remain available?

Does M5 establish actual Projects entry, or only its checked account state and department value?

5. Separate Jordan's completed and failed outcomes

Use L1 to L5. What is confirmed for Okta and AD? What failed for Projects, and what is still unknown about that failure? Explain why reactivating Jordan is not an appropriate shortcut.

6. Interpret the lingering session

L6 shows a new authenticated Expense response through Jordan's existing session after completed Okta deactivation. What does this prove? What does it not explain? Name the next evidence and outcome needed without assuming a session mechanism.

7. Write the handoff

Write one short handoff for Devi and one for Jordan. Each should state the approved outcome, completed changes, remaining discrepancy, responsible owner or next action, and closure evidence. Then explain why a later successful Projects deactivation would not close Jordan's entire case by itself. Keep any proposed correction separate from a result actually supplied by the packet.
